The science you thought you knew.

GetClever2 is a YouTube channel of short, carefully fact-checked explanations of everyday physics and the misconceptions most of us carry. Each video starts with what people usually believe, shows where it breaks, and explains what is really going on. Sources for every claim are listed in each video's description.

The GetClever2 uploader

The channel is produced with a small private tool that uploads finished videos to the GetClever2 YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is used only by the channel's owner and is not offered to anyone else. See the privacy policy.
